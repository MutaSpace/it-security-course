Mission 06

Social Engineering Defense: help users recognize and report manipulation.

BrightFuture users report urgent messages, impersonation attempts, suspicious links, and requests for credentials or payments. Your team must analyze the patterns and create a practical awareness response.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 06

Situation

The attacker is trying to influence a decision before a control can help.

The safe sample set includes phishing, smishing, vishing, impersonation, business email compromise, pretexting, and other influence techniques. The goal is analysis and prevention, not sending or recreating harmful messages.

Mission objectives

  • Recognize common social-engineering channels, techniques, and influence principles.
  • Analyze sender, URL, language, context, attachment, and request indicators.
  • Choose safe reporting, verification, and escalation actions.
  • Create an awareness product that helps a specific audience make better decisions.

Do not send test messages, collect credentials, impersonate real people, or target anyone. Analyze only the safe materials provided for the course.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Use only instructor-provided samples and fictional identities.
  • 02Do not click live suspicious links or open unknown attachments.
  • 03Do not recreate credential forms or collect user responses.
  • 04Keep awareness examples clearly labeled as simulated and safe.
