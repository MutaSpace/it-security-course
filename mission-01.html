AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 01

Situation

The organization has no trusted security baseline.

Technology records are incomplete, sensitive information is not consistently classified, and leaders disagree about which systems require the most protection. Your team must organize the available evidence and recommend the first security priorities.

Mission objectives

  • Differentiate technical, managerial, operational, and physical controls.
  • Apply confidentiality, integrity, and availability to organization assets.
  • Identify critical assets, owners, classifications, and protection gaps.
  • Write an evidence-based risk-priority recommendation.

Use only the fictional scenario and instructor-approved files. Do not scan or test a real network.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Use only instructor-provided systems, records, and scenarios.
  • 02Do not scan or test a real organization network.
  • 03Record evidence before recommending changes.
  • 04Remove personal or private data from documentation.
