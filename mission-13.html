Mission 13

Backup and Recovery: prove that recovery is possible.

BrightFuture reports that backups are running, but recent restoration attempts were inconsistent and no one can show whether business recovery objectives are achievable. Your team must test the process.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 13

Situation

A backup is only useful when it can be restored within the required conditions.

The scenario includes critical and noncritical data, backup methods, storage locations, retention choices, snapshots, recovery dependencies, and business expectations for acceptable downtime and data loss.

Mission objectives

  • Differentiate backup, snapshot, replication, redundancy, archival, and recovery functions.
  • Apply RTO, RPO, MTTR, availability, and dependency reasoning.
  • Create or validate an approved backup and complete a controlled restoration test.
  • Document evidence, gaps, residual risk, and continuity improvements.

Use only assigned lab files and storage. Never overwrite the only copy of data, and do not connect personal or production cloud storage to the exercise.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Classify the test data and confirm the approved backup destination.
  • 02Preserve the original and verify the backup before testing recovery.
  • 03Use a separate restore location unless instructed otherwise.
  • 04Do not publish real backup contents, keys, paths, or sensitive records.
