Suggested use

External lab rule

Use only legal, provider-controlled environments.

Never transfer commands, targets, credentials, or attack techniques from a training platform to a real system without explicit authorization.

01

Check the scope

Know exactly which machine, account, or challenge is authorized.

02

Protect accounts

Use unique passwords and do not share private challenge credentials.

03

Sanitize evidence

Remove flags, tokens, private data, and restricted solutions from GitHub.

04

Ask first

Contact the instructor through email or Canvas when instructions are unclear.
