Portfolio standard

Show the process, not only the final answer.

A strong security artifact lets another analyst understand the scope, evidence, decision, action, test result, and remaining risk.

01

Context

State the problem, organization, scope, constraints, and expected outcome.

02

Evidence

Include useful screenshots, logs, diagrams, commands, observations, and results.

03

Reasoning

Explain why the evidence matters and why one action is preferable to another.

04

Communication

Provide technical detail and a concise summary for a nontechnical audience.

Quality check

Review every artifact before submission.

Use the same checklist for diagrams, reports, tickets, risk registers, change records, and briefings.

  • 01
    Scope is clear

    The reader knows what was reviewed and what was excluded.

  • 02
    Evidence supports the claim

    Every major conclusion can be traced to an observation or result.

  • 03
    Recommendations are actionable

    Actions have an owner, priority, outcome, and validation method.

  • 04
    Sensitive details are removed

    No real credentials, personal data, private IP details, or restricted information appear.

  • 05
    The artifact is readable

    Labels, headings, filenames, captions, and visual hierarchy support quick review.
