Documentation standard

A strong project tells a complete security story.

Each project should make sense to a reader who was not in the class. Explain why the work mattered, what evidence you used, what you changed or recommended, and how you validated the result.

Portfolio safety

Public work requires a final security review.

Treat your portfolio like a production release. Review every file, image, commit, and link before making the repository public.

SAFE TO PUBLISH
  • Fictional or sanitized scenarios
  • Your own explanations and reflections
  • Cropped screenshots with private details removed
  • Public tool documentation and official links
  • Diagrams you created from approved sample data
DO NOT PUBLISH
  • Passwords, tokens, keys, flags, or secrets
  • Grades, Canvas messages, or instructor feedback
  • Real personal or organization information
  • Private network details or real vulnerabilities
  • Restricted files, answer keys, or copied reports
