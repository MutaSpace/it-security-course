Mission 09

System Hardening: reduce the attack surface without breaking the mission.

A BrightFuture endpoint has unnecessary services, outdated software, permissive settings, weak local controls, and no trusted configuration baseline. Your team must harden it safely and prove that required functions still work.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 09

Situation

A secure configuration must be repeatable, testable, and recoverable.

The system supports a defined set of business tasks. Removing exposure is important, but an undocumented change that breaks service or cannot be reversed creates a different risk.

Mission objectives

  • Establish a baseline and identify unnecessary services, software, accounts, and permissions.
  • Apply patches, secure configurations, host protections, and least functionality.
  • Use change management, backups, snapshots, testing, and rollback planning.
  • Compare before and after evidence and document residual risk.

Harden only the assigned virtual machine or isolated lab system. Create a snapshot or approved recovery point before changing configuration.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Confirm the approved system and required services before making changes.
  • 02Record the original state and create a recovery point.
  • 03Apply one controlled change set at a time and test after each set.
  • 04Do not copy real credentials, keys, or restricted configuration data to GitHub.
