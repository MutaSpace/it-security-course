Mission 15

Capstone Security Assessment: integrate evidence across the full environment.

Your team receives a new BrightFuture branch or partner environment with architecture, access, vulnerability, data, monitoring, continuity, policy, and vendor weaknesses. You must deliver a complete security assessment.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 15

Situation

The capstone environment contains interacting weaknesses, not isolated quiz questions.

The package may include diagrams, assets, accounts, logs, vulnerability results, policies, backup evidence, help-desk records, and management requests. Your team must scope the work and connect findings across domains.

Mission objectives

  • Plan and conduct an authorized assessment using evidence from multiple security domains.
  • Prioritize weaknesses using technical severity, business impact, exposure, and control context.
  • Implement and validate selected defensive improvements in the approved lab.
  • Produce technical findings, an executive summary, evidence, change records, and portfolio documentation.

Use only the assigned capstone environment and evidence. The scope, allowed techniques, required outputs, and scenario variation are provided in Canvas.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Confirm scope, roles, evidence sources, and stopping conditions before work begins.
  • 02Preserve original artifacts and keep a decision and change log.
  • 03Do not use destructive techniques or test anything outside the assigned lab.
  • 04Publish only the sanitized portfolio version approved for public release.
