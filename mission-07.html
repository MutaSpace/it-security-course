Mission 07

Suspicious Activity Investigation: turn scattered evidence into a defensible timeline.

BrightFuture receives an unusual login report, endpoint alert, DNS anomaly, and help-desk ticket that may be related. Your team must determine what the evidence supports and what still needs investigation.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 07

Situation

Several weak signals may describe one event, or several unrelated events.

The evidence set contains logs, alert summaries, packet details, timestamps, and user reports. Some fields are incomplete, clocks may not align, and not every alert represents malicious activity.

Mission objectives

  • Preserve and organize investigation evidence before changing the environment.
  • Normalize timestamps and correlate events across multiple sources.
  • Identify indicators, affected assets, likely sequence, and confidence level.
  • Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, unsupported assumptions, and unanswered questions.

Investigate only instructor-provided evidence and isolated lab data. Do not attempt attribution or access any real account, device, or external system.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Preserve originals and work from copies.
  • 02Record time zone, source, and collection context for each item.
  • 03Do not change evidence to make the narrative cleaner.
  • 04Escalate uncertainty and possible sensitive data exposure immediately.
