Mission 04

Identity and Access Audit: give the right access to the right people.

BrightFuture has shared credentials, stale accounts, inconsistent group membership, and users with more privilege than their roles require. Your team must redesign access without interrupting legitimate work.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 04

Situation

Access has accumulated faster than it has been reviewed.

The user roster, department roles, permissions, and account status records reveal terminated users, privilege creep, weak administrative separation, and inconsistent onboarding and offboarding practices.

Mission objectives

  • Differentiate identification, authentication, authorization, and accounting.
  • Apply least privilege, need to know, separation of duties, and role-based access.
  • Evaluate MFA, privileged access, service accounts, and account lifecycle controls.
  • Document access decisions and verify that intended users can still complete required tasks.

Use only fictional accounts or instructor-approved lab accounts. Never change permissions on a real organization account or system.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Work only with the provided identity records and isolated lab directory.
  • 02Create a rollback point before changing permissions.
  • 03Test both allowed and denied access using approved accounts.
  • 04Sanitize usernames, email addresses, and screenshots before publishing.
