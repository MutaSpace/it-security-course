Mission 14

Risk and Governance: translate security evidence into leadership decisions.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 14

Situation

Leadership needs clear choices, not an unranked list of problems.

The evidence set includes asset criticality, incidents, vulnerabilities, audit observations, vendor information, policies, contracts, control gaps, and financial or operational constraints.

Mission objectives

  • Write complete risk statements connecting assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.
  • Use qualitative and approved quantitative methods to prioritize risk.
  • Evaluate treatment options, controls, policy, compliance, audit, and third-party considerations.
  • Communicate recommendations, ownership, residual risk, and decision needs to leadership.

Use the fictional organization data and approved assumptions. This exercise is educational and does not provide legal, regulatory, or financial advice.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01State assumptions, scoring scales, evidence, and uncertainty.
  • 02Do not claim compliance based on an incomplete classroom review.
  • 03Separate inherent risk, control effectiveness, and residual risk.
  • 04Sanitize contracts, names, prices, and internal details before publishing.
