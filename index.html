Course overview

You are not just taking a class.
You are joining a security team.

This course provides instruction in security for network hardware, software, and data, including physical security, backup procedures, security tools, encryption, and protection from malicious software. Concepts are learned through practical missions aligned to CompTIA Security+.

01

Defend

Harden systems, secure networks, manage access, and apply layered security controls.

02

Investigate

Analyze alerts, logs, traffic, vulnerabilities, and evidence to determine what happened.

03

Analyze

Prioritize risk, compare controls, document findings, and make evidence-based decisions.

04

Respond

Contain incidents, recover operations, communicate clearly, and improve future defenses.

Mission 01: Security Team Onboarding

Active mission
CONFIDENTIAL // STUDENT ANALYST AUG 2026

Situation report

BrightFuture Learning Center has hired your team to assess its security posture.

The organization has grown quickly, but its technology inventory is incomplete and no one agrees on which systems or data are most critical. Your team must establish a trustworthy baseline before security improvements can begin.

Mission objectives

  • Identify hardware, software, users, and data assets
  • Classify assets by sensitivity and business importance
  • Recognize administrative, technical, physical, and operational controls
  • Submit an initial risk-priority recommendation

Analyst tip
