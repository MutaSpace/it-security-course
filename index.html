Course experience

You are joining a security team, not just reading a textbook.

The course teaches the concepts behind CompTIA Security+ through investigations, configuration tasks, audits, risk decisions, documentation, and incident scenarios. The same course hub supports online and in-person sections.

01

Defend

Harden systems, secure networks, manage access, and apply layered controls.

02

Investigate

Analyze alerts, logs, traffic, vulnerabilities, and evidence to explain what happened.

03

Analyze

Prioritize risk, compare controls, document findings, and make evidence-based decisions.

04

Communicate

Turn technical evidence into reports, change records, portfolios, and leadership briefings.

How every mission works

Brief. Learn. Operate. Debrief.

Each mission follows a repeatable workflow so you can focus on solving the security problem.
