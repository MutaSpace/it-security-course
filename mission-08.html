Mission 08

Vulnerability Assessment: validate findings before prioritizing remediation.

BrightFuture receives a scanner report with critical, high, medium, and informational findings. Your team must determine which findings are valid, which matter most, and how remediation should be verified.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 08

Situation

A scanner result is evidence to investigate, not an automatic decision.

The report includes exposed services, missing patches, weak configurations, outdated software, and possible false positives. Business criticality, exploitability, exposure, compensating controls, and change risk affect priority.

Mission objectives

  • Differentiate scanning, assessment, validation, penetration testing, and audit activities.
  • Confirm findings using safe evidence from the isolated lab or provided report set.
  • Prioritize vulnerabilities using severity plus business and environmental context.
  • Recommend remediation, mitigation, acceptance, transfer, or avoidance and verify the result.

Scan only the isolated target explicitly provided for this mission, or analyze the supplied scan report. Never scan an external address or real organization system.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Confirm written authorization and target scope before any scan.
  • 02Use conservative settings and the instructor-approved tool or report.
  • 03Do not exploit findings unless a separate approved exercise explicitly requires it.
  • 04Sanitize hostnames, addresses, screenshots, and course-specific answers before publishing.
