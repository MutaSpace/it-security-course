Mission 03

Network Redesign: reduce unnecessary trust and exposure.

BrightFuture uses a flat network where users, servers, management interfaces, guest devices, and sensitive data share too much trust. Your team must design a safer architecture without breaking required services.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 03

Situation

The current network makes one compromise too powerful.

The provided diagram shows weak segmentation, exposed management paths, unclear wireless boundaries, and protective controls placed without a clear purpose. Business services still need to remain available after the redesign.

Mission objectives

  • Identify trust boundaries, data flows, exposed services, and single points of failure.
  • Create appropriate zones, segments, and controlled communication paths.
  • Place firewalls, filtering, monitoring, and availability controls where they reduce risk.
  • Explain security, usability, cost, and availability tradeoffs in the proposed design.

This is a defensive architecture exercise. Use only the fictional diagram and approved simulation tools. Do not map or probe a real network.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Preserve the original network diagram for comparison.
  • 02Base every control placement on a documented risk or data flow.
  • 03Keep legitimate business communication working in the proposed design.
  • 04Do not include real network addresses or confidential diagrams in GitHub.
