Mission 11

Network Defense: analyze traffic and enforce safer communication.

BrightFuture sees unusual DNS and web traffic crossing network boundaries. Your team must analyze the approved packet capture and design controls that reduce risk while preserving legitimate communication.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 11

Situation

Network evidence shows communication, but context determines whether it is expected.

The packet capture and diagram include normal business traffic, misconfiguration, and suspicious patterns. Proposed firewall, ACL, filtering, IDS, or IPS decisions must be testable and narrowly scoped.

Mission objectives

  • Analyze packet metadata, protocols, endpoints, ports, flags, DNS, and application clues.
  • Differentiate expected, unusual, unauthorized, and malicious-looking traffic using evidence.
  • Write firewall or ACL logic that follows least privilege and rule-order principles.
  • Compare IDS, IPS, filtering, segmentation, and endpoint controls, including limitations.

Analyze only instructor-provided packet captures and isolated lab traffic. Do not capture or inspect traffic from a real network or another person.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Use only the provided capture, diagram, and approved lab endpoints.
  • 02Do not collect live traffic outside the isolated exercise.
  • 03Write rules for the stated business need, not broad blocking without analysis.
  • 04Sanitize addresses, hostnames, payloads, and course-specific indicators before publishing.
