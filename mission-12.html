Mission 12

Incident Response: contain the event while preserving what matters.

BrightFuture faces a simulated account compromise, ransomware event, data exposure, or other incident selected for your section. Your team must coordinate triage, evidence, containment, recovery, and communication.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 12

Situation

The response must reduce harm without destroying evidence or creating new risk.

The scenario develops through alerts, user reports, logs, affected assets, and new information. The team must make time-sensitive decisions, document assumptions, and update the plan as evidence changes.

Mission objectives

  • Apply preparation, detection, analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned.
  • Classify severity, scope, business impact, evidence needs, and communication requirements.
  • Select containment actions that balance risk reduction, evidence preservation, and operations.
  • Produce an incident timeline, report, recovery plan, and lessons-learned improvement actions.

This is a controlled simulation. Use only the scenario evidence and assigned lab systems. Do not deploy malware, access real accounts, or interact with external targets.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Follow the incident role and authority assigned in Canvas.
  • 02Preserve evidence before changing affected lab systems when safe to do so.
  • 03Record each decision, owner, time, evidence, and expected result.
  • 04Escalate legal, privacy, safety, or scope concerns immediately.
