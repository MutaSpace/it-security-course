Mission 02

Asset Discovery: know what must be protected.

BrightFuture Learning Center has conflicting hardware, software, account, cloud, data, and physical security records. Your team must create a trusted inventory before the organization can prioritize protection.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 02

Situation

The organization cannot protect assets it cannot account for.

Procurement lists, help-desk records, user interviews, cloud dashboards, and physical observations do not agree. Some assets have no documented owner, some data has no classification, and several systems may be missing from the official inventory.

Mission objectives

  • Inventory hardware, software, identities, cloud services, data, and supporting physical assets.
  • Assign an owner, location, criticality, lifecycle state, and classification to each asset.
  • Reconcile duplicate, incomplete, or conflicting records using documented evidence.
  • Identify asset-management and physical-security gaps that create risk.

Use only instructor-provided records, images, and isolated lab systems. Do not scan a real network or collect information from an organization without authorization.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Use only the provided inventory evidence and approved observation area.
  • 02Record the source for every important inventory decision.
  • 03Label unknown values and assumptions instead of inventing information.
  • 04Remove personal, private, or identifying data before publishing.
