AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 05

Situation

Sensitive data exists in several states with different risks.

The scenario includes files at rest, web traffic in transit, information being processed, backup copies, and integrity-sensitive records. A control that protects one state may not protect another.

Mission objectives

  • Differentiate hashing, encryption, encoding, obfuscation, signing, and tokenization.
  • Compare symmetric and asymmetric cryptography and explain where each fits.
  • Inspect certificates, trust relationships, expiration, names, and common validation concerns.
  • Recommend protections for data at rest, in transit, and in use, including key-management needs.

Use only instructor-provided test files, certificates, and keys. Never publish private keys, passwords, tokens, or real sensitive data.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Work only with disposable sample data and lab keys.
  • 02Preserve original files and record hashes before making changes.
  • 03Do not copy keys or encrypted course files to public repositories.
  • 04Explain the purpose and limitations of every cryptographic control.
