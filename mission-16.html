Mission 16

Incident Injection and Executive Defense: adapt, respond, and defend the decisions.

During the capstone, your team receives an unexpected incident, failed control, vendor alert, account compromise, or recovery problem. You must respond without losing control of the larger assessment.

AUTHORIZED COURSE SCENARIOMISSION 16

Situation

The final challenge tests decision-making when the plan changes.

The injection adds new evidence and may change scope, severity, priorities, communication, or recovery needs. The team must update its conclusions, document decisions, and brief an executive audience.

Mission objectives

  • Triage and respond to a new event while preserving evidence and assessment integrity.
  • Update risk, containment, recovery, communication, and remediation decisions as facts change.
  • Explain uncertainty, tradeoffs, residual risk, and recommended next actions.
  • Complete the final GitHub portfolio review and deliver an executive defense of the team work.

The incident injection and executive questions are provided in Canvas. Use only the assigned scenario and lab evidence, and stop if scope or safety becomes unclear.

Rules of engagementRequired
  • 01Record the injection time, source, evidence, and changed assumptions.
  • 02Preserve evidence before modifying affected lab systems when safe.
  • 03Assign communication ownership and keep technical and executive updates consistent.
  • 04Do not publish the hidden injection, answer key, private feedback, or restricted evidence.
